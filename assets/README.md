# Ferret logo and branding

`logo.png` and `logo-transparent.png` are the Ferret logo (the two files are currently identical). The
application icons under `packages/ferret-electron/resources/icons`, the favicon and the About image are
resized copies of it.

## Origin

The logo was generated with ChatGPT image generation by [@jakahann](https://github.com/jakahann) in 2026.
It is not based on Bruno's logo or branding.

The Ferret name and logo are **not** covered by the MIT license that applies to the code.

- You are welcome to use them to refer to this project, for example in an article or when saying that your
  software is "based on Ferret".

  Ferret.
